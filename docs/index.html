How It Works

Four simple steps from setup to offer tracking.

1

Configure

Add your LinkedIn credentials, resume, and job preferences to config.yaml.

2

Scan

The agent monitors LinkedIn 24/7 and scores new postings against your profile.

3

Apply

High-scoring jobs get applied to automatically via Easy Apply, with human-in-the-loop for sensitive fields.

4

Track

Every application appears on your Kanban board. Drag cards as you progress through stages.
